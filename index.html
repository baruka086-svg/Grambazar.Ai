1rem margin-md: 1.5rem margin-lg: 2.5rem space-xs: 0.25rem space-sm: 0.5rem space-md: 1rem space-lg: 1.5rem space-xl: 2rem --- ## Brand & Style This design system establishes an authentic, premium rural-modern commerce identity for GramBazaar in Shahdol, Madhya Pradesh. The visual direction balances the dependability of local heritage with the polish and frictionless clarity of world-class digital storefronts. ### Visual Style - **Rural-Modern Elegance**: Replaces the clutter and transactional sterility of typical hyper-local marketplaces with breathing room, warm organic undertones, and tactile card modules. - **Warm Earth & Lush Canopy**: An earthy terracotta and forest emerald core anchors the digital market to fertile regional agriculture and honest craftsmanship. - **High Trust & Direct Utility**: Prominent verification badges, instant localized messaging access (WhatsApp integration), and legible Hindi-English typographic pairing instill immediate confidence across both urban and rural demographics. ## Colors The palette draws directly from the agricultural heartland of Shahdol: dense sal forests, clay tile roofs, and warm grain stores. ### Palette Architecture - **Primary (`#1B4D3E`)**: Deep Forest Emerald. Represents reliability, life, and market stability. Applied to major actions, primary CTAs, active states, and structural top-level bars. - **Secondary (`#C86D38`)**: Warm Earthy Terracotta. Applied to special offers, regional craft highlights, notification chips, and primary price accents. - **Tertiary (`#25D366`)**: Verified Messaging Green. Reserved exclusively for direct WhatsApp inquiry buttons, live vendor communication, and instantaneous dispatch indicators. - **Neutral Primary (`#1C2826`)**: Deep Slate Charcoal. Formulated with a minute forest undertone to soften contrast against cream surfaces while maintaining strict WCAG AAA readability. - **Neutral Muted (`#52605B`)**: Subdued foliage slate for secondary metadata, unit metrics, and helper labels. - **Background (`#FBF9F4`)**: Warm Raw Cream canvas that reduces glare and prevents the sterile clinical feel of stark white. - **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp card containers that elevate product items effortlessly against the cream canvas. - **Borders & Dividers (`#D1DED6`)**: Soft sage outlines ensuring subtle structural bounding without visual noise. ## Typography Typography focuses on high-legibility geometric clarity with friendly humanist warmth. ### Bilingual Harmony - Font selection must render cleanly in both English and Devanagari script configurations via system fallbacks to Noto Sans Devanagari or Mukta. - Generous line-height values accommodate Devanagari matras (vowel diacritics) and conjuncts without accidental vertical clipping. - Currency symbols (₹) sit baseline-aligned with strict matching weights to prevent visual imbalance in pricing blocks. ## Layout & Spacing GramBazaar uses a mobile-first fluid responsive grid anchored by generous touch paddings for handheld shopping in vibrant outdoor market conditions. ### Breakpoints & Columns - **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Product cards default to 2-column grids (or full-width horizontal cards). - **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout, `margin-md: 1.5rem`, `gutter-md: 1.25rem`. Product cards scale to 3-column grids. - **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at `1280px` max-width, `margin-lg: 2.5rem`, `gutter-lg: 1.5rem`. Side-nav and filter trays stay anchored alongside 4-column product displays. ### Touch Ergonomics All interactive controls adhere to a strict minimum touch target of 48px × 48px to support effortless operation across diverse mobile hardware. ## Elevation & Depth Visual hierarchy uses soft ambient diffusion tinted with warm forest and terracotta undertones, avoiding cold synthetic gray drop shadows. ### Surface Levels - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background layer (`#FBF9F4`). - **Level 1 (Card Default)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with low-contrast sage perimeter border (`1px solid #D1DED6`) and a diffused warm shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.05), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.04);` - **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product Cards)**: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(200, 109, 56, 0.04);` - **Level 3 (Sticky Trays & Bottom Sheets)**: `box-shadow: 0 -4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.06);` - **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays)**: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -6px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.16);` ## Shapes The shape system employs soft, welcoming geometries that convey approachable community commerce. ### Radius Scale - **Small (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Badges, price pills, form inputs, counter buttons. - **Medium (`1rem` / 16px)**: Standard product cards, vendor profiles, promo banners. - **Large (`1.25rem` / 20px)**: Featured hero cards, category showcases, bottom sheets. - **Pill (`9999px`)**: WhatsApp CTAs, primary action pills, and status tags. ## Components ### Buttons - **Primary CTA**: Background `#1B4D3E`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px` or `0.75rem`, minimum height `48px`. Pressed state transitions to `#13382D`. - **Secondary CTA**: Background `#F4EFE6`, foreground `#1B4D3E`, border `1px solid #D1DED6`. - **WhatsApp Action**: Background `#25D366`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by WhatsApp icon. Height `48px`, font-weight `600`. Hover state gently elevates with `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.3)`. ### Cards & Marketplace Listings - **Product Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D1DED6`, border-radius `1rem`. Internal image container features an aspect ratio of `1:1` or `4:3` with background `#F4EFE6`. - **Pricing Cluster**: Current price highlighted in `price-lg` font weight with `#1B4D3E` (or `#C86D38` when discounted). Original MRP is rendered in `body-sm` with a strikethrough in `#52605B`. - **Vendor & Origin Tag**: Micro-badge anchored below title: subtle terracotta tint `#FBF2EC`, text `#C86D38`, border-radius `4px`, displaying "Shahdol Mandi" or verified local producer. ### Chips & Badges - **Organic / Farm Fresh Badge**: Forest Green pill (`#EAF2EE`), text `#1B4D3E`, font size `12px`, bold. - **Artisan / Handcrafted Badge**: Warm Terracotta pill (`#FAECE4`), text `#C86D38`, font size `12px`, bold. - **Filter Chips**: Unselected state features white surface with `#D1DED6` border. Selected state inverts to `#1B4D3E` with crisp white text. ### Form Inputs & Selectors - **Input Fields**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D1DED6`, border-radius `0.75rem`, height `48px`, font size `16px` to prevent iOS auto-zoom. Focus ring uses `2px solid #1B4D3E` with offset. - **Bilingual Support Prompt**: Contextual floating language switcher (EN / हिंदी) positioned in search and global headers. ### Quantity Selectors & Floating Cart - **Counter Control**: Compact capsule with minus, numerical count, and plus controls in `#F4EFE6` background, surrounded by `#D1DED6` border, preventing duplicate touches. - **Sticky Quick-Cart Bar**: Floats docked to mobile viewport bottom with subtle blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(251, 249, 244, 0.9)`), offering an instant summary of cart total and direct order confirmation.
